Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Why does my password not show up while typing in the installer?

A: This is completely normal! Linux hides password characters (no dots or stars) for security reasons. Just type your password blindly and press Enter.

Q: How do I select my city during timezone setup?

A: You can scroll through the list using your keyboard arrow keys. Once you find your city's number, press the letter q to lock your choice.

Q: Where can I check system logs after an error?

A: Memora OS features automated CLI session recording. You can check your logs inside /var/log/memora_cli.log.

Q: How do I contribute to the Memora project?

A: You can visit our Codeberg repository or check out the Developer Documentation page for source tree guidelines and ISO building scripts.
